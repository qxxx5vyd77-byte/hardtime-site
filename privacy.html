Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 — Hard Time: Prison Workout

Hard Time collects no data. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything the app knows about you lives on your phone.

Workout and progress data

Your workout history, daily counts, streaks, program progress, and placement-quiz answers are stored only on your device (and in your device backups). They are never uploaded, shared, or seen by us — the app has no server.

Notifications

If you turn on Yard Calls, the app schedules local reminders on your device. These never leave your phone, and you can turn them off any time in Settings.

Network requests

The app makes no network requests during normal use. If you choose to rate the app, Apple's standard in-app rating dialog is provided by iOS and governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

The share cards you create (Time Served and Released posters) are generated on your device and only go where you choose to send them.

Children

The app is rated for ages 13 and up and collects no data from anyone.

Changes
